# KuCoin Exchange Hack (2020): Blockchain Forensic Investigation

First Quadrant Labs, Project 2 (digital forensics).

## Overview
An investigation of the 2020 hack of the KuCoin cryptocurrency exchange, in which about $275M in assets was stolen. The work reconstructs the attack timeline, traces the stolen funds and attributes the attack.

## Key findings
- Reconstructed the attack timeline and traced $275M in stolen assets across 7+ blockchains, including Bitcoin and Ethereum.
- Attributed the attack to the Lazarus Group using OSINT and MITRE ATT&CK.
- Verified every finding against at least two independent sources.
- Kept factual evidence separate from interpretation, with a chain of custody.
- Identified a 4 to 5 hour detection delay and recommended cold storage, multi-signature wallets, HSM key management, real-time alerting and an incident response plan.
